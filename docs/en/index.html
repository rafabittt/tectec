Features

Small on the outside, crafted on the inside.

A native, light, quiet app that does one thing and does it well.

Real recordings, not synthesis

Every switch was recorded pressing and releasing, with its own sound for each row, Space, Enter and Backspace.

Sound with a position

The sound comes from where the key is: Q on the left, Enter on the right. Made for the Brazilian ABNT2 layout, too.

Q⏎

Lives in the menu bar

Native Swift, light and instant. No window, no Dock icon.

Listens, never keeps

It hears keys to play a sound and nothing else. Nothing is recorded or sent, and password fields never reach it.

Updates itself

Signed and notarized by Apple. When a new version ships, TecTec tells you and installs it.

Never sounds repetitive

Every press varies the pitch a little, like a real keyboard. Release sounds can be turned off.

Bring your own sounds

Import Mechvibes packs or a folder of per-key WAVs and they show up in the menu.

Install

Three steps and you're done.

Download and drag

Open the DMG and drag TecTec to Applications.

Grant permission

In System Settings › Privacy & Security › Input Monitoring, turn TecTec on.

Pick your switch

Click the Ç in the menu bar and choose. That's it: tec, tec, tec.
